Get help

Found a bug, a wrong crop, or have an idea? Send us a note. Please include your state and roughly where you were.

Common questions

Where does the crop data come from?

From USDA satellite crop maps. Where this season's in-season map is available, you'll see a gold LIVE tag. Elsewhere, Spot-a-Crop uses the latest yearly USDA map and the field's crop rotation history.

Why does it say “Finding GPS…”?

Make sure Location is on: Settings › Spot-a-Crop › Location. Choose Always if you want voice to keep naming crops with the screen locked.

Voice isn't talking

Tap the speaker button at the top, and check that your phone isn't muted and the volume is up.

Is it safe to use while driving?

Passengers can read the screen. Drivers should turn on voice and keep their eyes on the road.

Does it use a lot of data?

Some, like any map app. Areas you've seen are saved for later, and on a weak signal the app loads lighter maps.

How do I replay the intro?

Open the ☰ menu and tap How Spot-a-Crop works.
