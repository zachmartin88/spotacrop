## Description
Ever drive past miles of fields and wonder what's growing? Spot-a-Crop tells you, out loud.

Tap Start driving and the app names the crops on your left and right as you go: corn, soybeans, wheat, cotton, rice, orchards, vineyards and dozens more, anywhere in the lower 48.

PICK A CO-PILOT
Choose who calls out the crops. Earl the Southern farmer. Dot the sweet grandma. Buck the rodeo announcer. Penny the pun machine. Nigel the nature-documentary narrator. Detective Rye, Coach Hank, Brody the surfer, Delphine the drama queen, Kernel the corn kid, or calm, clear Sam. Each has over a thousand lines, so they rarely repeat. Or let them take turns.
• Works with your screen locked and your music playing
• Downloads once, then works with no signal
• Welcomes you to each state, cracks jokes on long stretches, and recaps your drive

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM
USDA satellite crop maps. Where this season's map is available you'll see a gold LIVE tag. Everywhere else it uses the latest yearly USDA map plus each field's crop history, with official USDA field outlines.

TAP ANY FIELD
• What's growing and how many acres
• What was planted there in past years
• This week's USDA harvest progress for that crop in your state
• Fun facts about the crop

ROAD-TRIP FUN
• Collect a crop card for every crop you drive past. Rare ones glow.
• Road-trip bingo: a new card every day
• Guess the crop from a bird's-eye photo
• Badges, state stamps and a leaderboard

PLAN A DRIVE
See what you'll pass on the way, and save the route for stretches with no signal.

No account and no ads. Your trips, cards and badges stay on your phone. Character voices are AI voices.

Drivers: use voice and keep your eyes on the road. Passengers can read the screen.

## App Review notes
Spot-a-Crop names the crops in fields beside the road using USDA satellite crop maps.
No sign-in is needed.
To test without driving: tap "Explore map" and tap any field. In Iowa, Illinois or Nebraska most fields are corn or soybeans.
Background location and audio are used only while "Start driving" is on, so the chosen character voice can keep naming crops with the screen locked. Location stops when you tap End drive, or automatically after about 10 minutes parked.
Voices: tap the speaker, pick a character (downloads about 20 MB from our site), then Start driving. Voices are AI-generated with open-source models and are credited in the app (☰ › About the data).
