## Description
Ever drive past miles of fields and wonder what's growing? Spot-a-Crop tells you.

Tap Start driving and the top bar names the crops on your left and right as you go: corn, soybeans, wheat, cotton, rice, orchards, vineyards and dozens more, anywhere in the lower 48. Turn on voice and it reads them out loud, even with your screen locked.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM
Spot-a-Crop uses USDA satellite crop maps. Where this season's map is available you'll see a gold LIVE tag. Everywhere else it uses the latest yearly USDA map plus each field's crop history.

TAP ANY FIELD
• What's growing and how many acres
• What was planted there the past five years
• This week's USDA harvest progress for that crop in your state
• Fun facts about the crop

ROAD-TRIP FUN
• Collect a crop card for every crop you drive past. Rare ones glow.
• Road-trip bingo: a new card every day
• Guess the crop from a bird's-eye photo
• Badges, state stamps and a leaderboard
• Meet Kernel, your corn-cob co-pilot

PLAN A DRIVE
See what you'll pass on the way, and save the route for stretches with no signal.

SHARE YOUR FINDS
Turn any field into a postcard for friends.

No account and no ads. Your trips, cards and badges stay on your phone.

Drivers: please use voice and keep your eyes on the road. Passengers can read the screen.

## App Privacy answers ("nutrition label")
Data collected:
- **Location > Precise location**: App Functionality. Not linked to the user. Not used for tracking.
- **Identifiers > Device ID** (a random ID the app makes for the leaderboard): App Functionality. Not linked to the user. Not used for tracking.
- **Other data > Gameplay content** (leaderboard nickname and totals): App Functionality. Not linked to the user. Not used for tracking.

Tracking: No.

## App Review notes
Spot-a-Crop names the crops in fields beside the road using USDA satellite crop maps.
No sign-in is needed.
To test without driving: tap "Explore map" and tap any field. In Iowa, Illinois or Nebraska most fields are corn or soybeans.
Background location and audio are used only while "Start driving" is on, so voice can keep naming crops with the screen locked. The app stops using location when the drive ends.
