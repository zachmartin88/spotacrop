Spot-a-Crop

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold. Your location is used only to look up the crops around you, and your trips, cards and badges stay on your phone.

Location

Spot-a-Crop uses your location to show what's growing in the fields near you. When you tap Start driving, the app reads your location while you drive. With your permission, it keeps reading it while the screen is locked, so voice can keep naming crops.

To draw the map and name the crops, the app requests map and crop data for the area around you from the public services listed below. Those requests include the map area being viewed, much like any map app. Our small relay server passes some of these requests along and briefly caches the map images. It doesn't save your location, link requests to you, or keep a history of where you've been.

When you first open the website or app, a rough location (city level) may be estimated from your internet address so the map starts near you.

What stays on your phone

Your crop cards, badges, stats, trip log, settings and leaderboard nickname are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Leaderboard

To rank you on the leaderboard, the app sends your nickname (a random fun name unless you change it), a random device ID, and your totals (cards, states, badges, miles) to our server when it opens and as your totals change. No location, name, email or contact info is sent. Don't use your real name as a nickname if you don't want it shown.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily crop reminder, the reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

Sharing

Share cards are made on your phone. They're only sent where you choose to share them.

Data sources and services

Spot-a-Crop gets map and crop data from these public services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • USDA NASS Cropland Data Layer and Crop Progress, served by George Mason University's CSISS
  • USDA Crop Sequence Boundaries
  • Esri basemaps and boundaries, and state and county parcel map services
  • U.S. Census geocoder
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (place search) and OSRM (routes)
  • GeoJS (approximate starting location)
  • Render, which hosts our small data relay and leaderboard

Children

Spot-a-Crop is for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.
